## Market Outlook
Near-term outlook is neutral-to-cautious. Bitcoin’s leadership and 60.0% dominance indicate market concentration that can limit broad-based altcoin rallies unless BTC weakness opens windows for alt rotation. Watch the following:
- Volume and liquidity metrics on top movers (especially JCT and RITUAL) to assess sustainability.
- Net flows into exchanges and stablecoins as a gauge of risk appetite.
- Sector-specific news (partnerships, protocol upgrades, listings) that could extend rallies for microcaps.
- Macro headlines and regulatory developments that can quickly shift risk sentiment.

A breakout in either direction from Bitcoin would likely be the catalyst for a broader market trend change. Until then, expect episodic, high-volatility moves primarily among smaller-cap tokens.
