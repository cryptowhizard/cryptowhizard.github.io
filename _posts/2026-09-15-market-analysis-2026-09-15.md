With BTC at $78456 and ETH at $2504.91, the top two assets continue to set the tone: large-cap stability appears to be encouraging traders to selectively take risk into smaller, higher-beta tokens while still keeping allocation anchored in BTC/ETH.

- Derivatives metrics: Watch implied volatility and open interest per asset for clues on leverage-driven squeezes or capitulation risks.
