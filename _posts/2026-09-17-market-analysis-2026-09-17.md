## Executive Summary
The crypto market is in a cautious, risk-off phase as total market capitalization stands at $2.59T and Bitcoin dominance sits at 58.5%. Bitcoin is trading at $75,759 and Ethereum at $2,394.7 while most sectors and altcoins are soft; Bedrock (BR) is the standout gainer (+128.11%) and RaveDAO (RAVE) the biggest decliner (-13.78%).

## Market Overview
The market-wide capitalization of $2.59 trillion and a relatively elevated Bitcoin dominance of 58.5% indicate investor preference for large-cap, liquid assets over smaller altcoins at this moment. Bitcoin at $75,759 and Ethereum at $2,394.7 remain the market anchors; the dominance reading suggests capital is rotating toward Bitcoin or staying in major tokens rather than distributing across altcoin risk. With the majority of tracked sectors showing negative 24-hour returns, the overall tone is defensive.
