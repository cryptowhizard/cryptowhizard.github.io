## Executive Summary
The crypto market shows a mixed session with total market capitalization at $2.76T while Bitcoin dominance rises to 59.4%, underscoring BTC-led strength. Bitcoin traded at $81,854 and Ethereum at $2,424.12 as risk appetite rotated: a handful of small-cap tokens posted double-digit gains while many sectors and large parts of the altcoin market slipped.

## Market Overview
- Total market cap: $2.76T — the market remains large-cap heavy.
- Bitcoin dominance: 59.4% — more than half the market cap is concentrated in Bitcoin.
- BTC price: $81,854; ETH price: $2,424.12.

Higher Bitcoin dominance alongside a stable-to-strong BTC price suggests capital continuing to flow into the largest, most liquid crypto asset. That dynamic typically compresses altcoin performance and increases relative volatility among smaller tokens, as seen in today’s dispersal between significant single-coin movers and broad sector weakness.

## Technical Analysis (Qualitative)
- Momentum: Bitcoin remains the dominant source of market momentum. The elevated dominance figure (59.4%) and a strong BTC price indicate bullish bias for the overall market’s risk-free benchmark, but not necessarily for altcoins.
- Trend strength: Trend for large-caps appears more resilient than for small-caps; many alts show weakening trend structure and increased fragmentation.
- Volatility & risk: Volatility is elevated in smaller-cap tokens (evidenced by double-digit gainers/losers). This increases execution and counterparty risk—important for leverage users.
- Trading guidance: Use volume-confirmed moves as a filter. Favor trades where breakout or breakdown is accompanied by above-average liquidity and on-chain confirmation (inflows/outflows to exchanges, active addresses). Keep position sizing conservative on low-liquidity names.

## Market Outlook — What to Watch
1. Bitcoin dominance trend: If dominance continues to climb, expect further altcoin underperformance and selective opportunities in high-quality L1/L2/DeFi projects only on clear on-chain or fundamental catalysts.  
2. Macro and regulatory headlines: Any major policy or regulatory announcements remain top drivers of cross-market volatility.  
3. On-chain flows: Exchange inflows/outflows, large wallet movements, and staking/unlocking schedules can quickly change supply dynamics for individual tokens.  
4. Sector rotations: Watch L2 and AI sectors closely for signs of capitulation or renewed inflows—these could be early indicators of a broader altcoin recovery.  
5. Volume confirmation: For short-term trades, require persistent volume to confirm price action and reduce false-breakout risk.

## Key Takeaways
