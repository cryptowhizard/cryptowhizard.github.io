Market cools as Bitcoin holds leadership while pockets of altcoin volatility steal headlines

The crypto market settled with a total market cap of $2.72T while Bitcoin maintained a dominant position at 59.4% dominance. BTC traded at $80,395 and ETH at $2,341.45 as selective altcoin moves produced outsized daily winners and losers, with Verified Emeralds (VEREM) surging and Manadia (UMXM) plunging.

## Market Overview
The total crypto market capitalization sits at $2.72 trillion, reflecting a broadly steady macro valuation for the sector. Bitcoin dominance of 59.4% indicates that capital remains concentrated in BTC relative to altcoins — a sign that risk appetite is tempered and investors are favoring perceived safety and liquidity. BTC is trading at $80,395 and ETH at $2,341.45; these levels suggest the market is anchored by the two largest assets while flows into smaller tokens remain idiosyncratic and headline-driven.

Volatility continues to be uneven: major-cap cryptocurrencies show more measured moves while small-cap tokens produce the most dramatic daily percentage swings. That dynamic increases event-driven risk for traders and highlights the importance of position sizing.
