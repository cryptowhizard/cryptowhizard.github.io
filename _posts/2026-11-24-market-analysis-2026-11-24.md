## Technical Analysis (Qualitative)
- Bitcoin: Trading at **$61,110**, BTC is showing selective strength but is susceptible to volatility around macro liquidity events. Momentum appears cautious-to-constructive; traders should watch for follow-through volume to validate any extension.
- Ethereum: At **$1,714.78**, ETH is maintaining relevance as the primary smart-contract settlement layer, but sector flows are not yet decisively favoring ETH over high-conviction L1 alternatives. Momentum is range-biased with episodic directional bursts.
- Altcoins: Extreme single-asset volatility (e.g., SFTMX) raises market-wide risk parameters. Many alt rallies are narrow; therefore, breadth metrics should guide risk appetite. When breadth is thin, alpha generation can be high but so is systemic drawdown risk.

Risk management advice: use position sizing, predefined exit rules, and trailing stops for volatile names. For swing trades, prefer assets with confirmed on-chain and off-chain catalysts and adequate liquidity. Maintain a clear timeframe — intraday traders can exploit momentum, while investors should focus on fundamentals and protocol durability.
