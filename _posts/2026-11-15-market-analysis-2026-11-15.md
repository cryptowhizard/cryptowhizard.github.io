## Executive Summary
The crypto market pulled back today with the total market cap at $2.22T and Bitcoin dominance steady at 58.7%. Bitcoin traded at $65,389 and Ethereum at $1,831.84 as risk-sensitive sectors (L1s, gaming, meme tokens) led declines while select small-cap projects posted outsized gains.

## Market Overview
Overall market conditions skewed negative on November 15, 2026. Total market capitalization stands at $2.22 trillion, reflecting a consolidation phase rather than broad expansion. Bitcoin dominance remains elevated at 58.7%, indicating capital is concentrated in BTC relative to altcoins. Bitcoin’s current price is $65,389 and Ethereum is trading at $1,831.84 — both levels suggest the majors are anchoring the market while many altcoins underperform.

The distribution of move sizes today points to elevated idiosyncratic volatility: large percentage swings in smaller-cap tokens occurred alongside a more muted action in the majors. That pattern is consistent with traders reallocating risk across pocketed opportunities rather than broad risk-on flows.
