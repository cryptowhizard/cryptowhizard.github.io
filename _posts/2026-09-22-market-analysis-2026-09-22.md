## Executive Summary
The crypto market showed broad risk-on behavior on September 22, 2026, with total market capitalization at $2.91T and Bitcoin dominance steady at 58.8%. Bitcoin and Ethereum traded at $85,764 and $2,745.90 respectively, while pockets of speculative strength pushed several small-cap tokens sharply higher—most notably GreenHood (HOOD) which surged 93.55%.

## Market Overview
Total market capitalization stands at $2.91 trillion, underscoring a sizeable liquid market that continues to support meaningful daily swings in individual tokens. Bitcoin dominance is 58.8%, indicating that Bitcoin remains the primary store of crypto market value and a key driver of sentiment. Bitcoin's spot price is $85,764 and Ethereum is at $2,745.90; these levels frame intraday risk appetite for many traders.

Breadth was constructive: multiple sectors recorded gains (DeFi, L1s, L2s, Gaming, RWA) and meme tokens posted outsized strength, suggesting a rotation into higher-beta assets alongside continued appetite for core assets. Overall, the data points to a market comfortable taking on risk, though concentrated movers and some large single-asset declines remind investors that idiosyncratic risk remains high.
