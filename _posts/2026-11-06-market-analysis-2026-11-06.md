## Executive Summary
The crypto market rallied with a notable sector rotation into AI tokens, lifting total market capitalization to $2.57T and sending Bitcoin dominance to 59.2%. Bitcoin traded at $75,809 and Ethereum at $2,248.71 as risk assets saw a bifurcated day: outsized gains in AI-focused coins versus broad weakness across DeFi, L1s and privacy tokens.

## Market Overview
Total crypto market cap stands at $2.57 trillion, with Bitcoin dominance at 59.2%, indicating that Bitcoin continues to command the majority share of market value. Bitcoin is trading at $75,809 and Ethereum at $2,248.71 — both figures suggest established leadership but the intra-market rotation toward high-beta sectors (notably AI) is reshaping short-term flows. Overall market behavior today reflects concentrated buying interest in a small subset of tokens while many traditional crypto sectors retraced.
