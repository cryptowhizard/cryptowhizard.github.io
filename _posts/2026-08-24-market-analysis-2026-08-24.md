## Technical Analysis (qualitative)
- Bitcoin at **$77,865** with dominance at **59.3%** signals that BTC remains the primary liquidity magnet. Momentum at the macro level appears constructive for risk assets, given the market cap figure and active altcoin moves, but concentration implies that sharp BTC moves would materially affect overall market direction.
- The small-cap spike in PONS, DOG, PROM and others reflects short-term explosive momentum driven by low liquidity rather than broad, sustainable accumulation. Expect elevated intraday volatility and frequent gap fills.
- Risk profile: elevated for small-cap altcoins (high upside, high downside). For large-caps (BTC, ETH), risk is concentrated in macro and liquidity shifts; momentum appears intact but vulnerable to swift reversals given the concentration.
- For traders: favor size discipline, tighter risk controls, and liquidity-aware execution (limit orders, staggered entries/exits). Avoid oversized position sizing in tokens with sharp one-day moves unless able to tolerate rapid drawdowns.

## Market Outlook — What to Watch
- Liquidity and flow signals: monitor exchange inflows/outflows for Bitcoin and top altcoins; large inflows can presage sell pressure.
- Sector rotation: watch whether DeFi and RWA leadership continues — sustained flows would imply a structural shift toward yield/utility narratives.
- Token-specific news: for names with explosive moves (PONS, PROM, DOG), verify listings, airdrops, partnerships, or on-chain whale activity that could underpin further moves or reversals.
- Macro/regulatory events: any major macro prints or regulatory developments can quickly reweight risk appetite; given BTC’s dominance, such events can cascade across markets.
- Volatility indicators: implied vol and on-chain activity should be monitored to time entries—high realized vol favors shorter trade horizons.
