Bitcoin dominance at 57.8% underscores a market structure where BTC retains a majority share of investor attention and capital allocation. Bitcoin's quoted price of $59,647 and Ethereum at $1,672.6 provide the reference frame: BTC is carrying market leadership while ETH's relative performance remains muted compared with select high-beta alts.

Market breadth is uneven — several microcaps posted outsized gains while select leveraged/tokenized products and privacy-focused projects led losses. Overall, the picture is one of consolidation with episodic volatility in smaller-cap, lower-liquidity names.
