## Technical Analysis (Qualitative)
- Bitcoin: Trading at $74,401. Price action suggests resilient momentum in the near term, with Bitcoin still controlling market risk appetite via dominance near 60%. Watch for volatility regimes to change if BTC experiences directional moves—altcoins typically amplify both ways.
- Ethereum: Trading at $2,089.17. ETH is acting as the secondary market anchor; its trend and gas demand dynamics will influence DeFi and L2 flows.
- Altcoin technical profile: High dispersion—several names seeing parabolic intraday patterns indicative of low-liquidity squeezes. Trend strength in high-flying tokens is strong but fragile; such trends can reverse quickly on liquidity shock or sell-the-news events.
- Risk levels: Elevated for small-cap altcoins due to order book thinness and concentrated holder bases. Correlation risk increases in a BTC sell-off; many altcoins historically exhibit amplified downside relative to Bitcoin.

Trade rules to consider:
- For traders: reduce position size in ultra-high-volatility names, favor liquid pairs, employ tight risk controls and plan exits.
- For investors: treat recent parabolic moves as speculative; require fundamental confirmation (adoption metrics, on-chain growth, treasury health) before increasing exposure.
