## Executive Summary
The crypto market showed selective risk-taking on August 18, with Total Market Cap at $2.28T and Bitcoin dominance steady at 56.5%. Bitcoin traded at $64,102 and Ethereum at $1,894.41 while small-cap altcoins produced outsized moves — Royal Euro (REUR) led gainers with a +262.80% surge. Layer-2s and gaming tokens lagged, signaling rotation into niche and real-world-asset (RWA) plays.

## Market Overview
The aggregate market capitalization sits at $2.28 trillion, reflecting a broadly constructive environment where liquidity is available but concentrated. Bitcoin dominance of 56.5% indicates BTC continues to command the majority of market value, yet the intraday activity shows capital rotating into selective alts rather than a wholesale altseason. BTC is trading at $64,102 and ETH at $1,894.41 — levels consistent with a market that is neither in panic nor euphoric expansion. Traders are balancing exposure to the relative safety of Bitcoin with targeted risk in smaller-cap tokens that can produce rapid returns (and rapid reversals).
