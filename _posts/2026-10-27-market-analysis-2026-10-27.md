## Market Outlook
Near term, expect continued selective risk-taking: investors will rotate capital between BTC/ETH (for stability) and microcaps (for alpha). Watch for:
- Any confirmatory news or listings that could sustain microcap rallies.
- Shifts in BTC dominance: continued readings near or above current levels would sustain large-cap leadership; a decline could presage broader altcoin rallies.
- Regulatory headlines, macro data, and major protocol updates that can quickly reprice risk assets.

For investors: maintain diversified exposures, monitor correlation between BTC/ETH and selected sectors, and prioritize capital preservation in smaller, more volatile tokens. For traders: exploit volatility with disciplined sizing and clearly defined stop-loss and take-profit rules.
